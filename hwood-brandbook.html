02 — Logo

Logo usage

The HWOOD wordmark is the primary brand identifier. Use it consistently on approved backgrounds. Do not modify, rotate, recolor, or distort the mark.

On light
On dark
On teal
❌ Do not
• Recolor the logo with any non-brand color
• Place on low-contrast backgrounds
• Stretch, rotate, or apply effects
• Use at sizes smaller than 24px height
✓ Do
• Use on white, #f8f8f8, #0a0a0a, or #005f5f
• Maintain minimum clear space = logo height × 0.5
• Invert to white on dark and teal backgrounds
• Request original PNG/SVG for new uses

08 — Patterns & Motion

Visual patterns

HWOOD uses a signature diagonal stripe motif derived from industrial manufacturing textures. It is used as a background decoration on dark sections and CTA banners — never on light backgrounds.

Diagonal stripes (signature pattern)
Two overlapping strips skewed at −20°. Teal (#005f5f) in front, dark teal (#004d4d) at 0.6 opacity behind. Only used on dark backgrounds.

10 — Brand Voice

Tone & language

HWOOD communicates like a precision instrument — concise, direct, and authoritative. We don't embellish. We don't oversell. We state what we do and how well we do it.

Tone attributes
Precise
Specific numbers, materials, and timelines. "72h lead time" not "fast turnaround".
Direct
Short sentences. Active voice. No filler phrases or vague adjectives.
Technical fluency
We speak CNC, cabinet, and workshop language naturally. B2B professionals are our audience.
Warm but not casual
Approachable in WhatsApp and support. Formal in contracts and specs.
Language
HWOOD communicates in English (primary) and Hebrew (parallel). RTL layout is fully supported. Both languages carry equal weight in the UI.
